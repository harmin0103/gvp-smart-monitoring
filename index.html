About GVP Watch

One platform for cleaner public spaces

GVP Watch is a smart waste-management prototype designed to connect citizens, municipal administrators and cleaning crews. Citizens can report sanitation concerns, administrators can review sample incidents and hotspots, and field teams can view and update assigned cleaning tasks.

  • Citizen reporting and complaint tracking
  • Central monitoring of sample incidents and hotspots
  • Cleaning task assignment and work-status updates
Smart Dustbin

A bin that responds when you approach

The concept uses a proximity sensor to detect a person nearby and automatically open the lid, helping people dispose of waste without touching the bin.

Approach the bin to open the lid

Move your pointer close to the bin, hover over it, or tap it on a touchscreen to try the demo.
